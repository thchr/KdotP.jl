Crystalline.timereversal_unitary — Method
timereversal_unitary(lgir::AbstractLGIrrep,
                     g₀::AbstractOperation = minus_k_operation(lgir),
                     αβγ::Union{AbstractVector{<:Real}, Nothing} = nothing)
                                                            --> Matrix{ComplexF64}

Return the unitary part Γ of the antiunitary operation 𝒯g₀ = ΓK of the gray little group of lgir, with K denoting complex conjugation, 𝒯 denoting time-reversal, and g₀ an operation of the space group that maps the k-point of lgir to minus itself (modulo a reciprocal lattice vector). Γ is given in the basis of lgir, evaluated at αβγ.

By default, g₀ is the operation returned by minus_k_operation: the identity if the k-point of lgir is a time-reversal invariant momentum (so that Γ is the unitary part of time-reversal itself), and otherwise the first operation of the space group (in the order of spacegroup) that maps k to -k. If no such operation exists, time-reversal is not an element of the gray little group, and an error is thrown. Note that Γ depends on the choice of g₀; to evaluate at a choice of αβγ, g₀ must be provided explicitly.

Γ obeys Γ*conj(D(g))*Γ' ≈ D(g₀gg₀⁻¹) for every g in the little group (with D(g) denoting the matrix of g in lgir(αβγ)) and Γ*conj(Γ) ≈ D((𝒯g₀)²), with (𝒯g₀)² = Ēg₀² for spinful irreps (Ē represented by -𝟙), and g₀² otherwise. Γ is determined up to an overall phase, which is fixed by requiring det(Γ) = 1.

The input irrep must be one that realify leaves unchanged: i.e., either an irrep that has already been glued together with its time-reversal partner (iscorep(lgir) = true) or one whose Reality type requires no gluing (REAL for spinless irreps, PSEUDOREAL for spinful ones).

KdotP.icosphere — Method
icosphere(subdivisions::Integer) --> (vertices, faces)

A triangulation of the unit sphere: an icosahedron with each face split into four, subdivisions times, and the new vertices projected onto the sphere. vertices is a vector of unit 3-vectors and faces a vector of index triples, oriented counter-clockwise as seen from outside. With s subdivisions it has 10·4^s + 2 vertices and 20·4^s faces.

KdotP.minimal_generators_indices — Method

Returns a set of indices into group g which generates the group g, i.e., such that

idxs = minimal_generators_indices(g; cntr)
g′ = generate(g[idxs]; cntr)

where g′ ~ g (but may differ by trivial reciprocal lattice vectors in some operations).

KdotP.minus_k_operation — Method
minus_k_operation(lg::AbstractLittleGroup)    --> Union{AbstractOperation, Nothing}
minus_k_operation(lgir::AbstractLGIrrep)      --> Union{AbstractOperation, Nothing}

Return an operation g₀ of the space group of the little group lg (or of the little group of lgir) that maps its k-point to minus itself, modulo a reciprocal lattice vector, i.e., with g₀k ≡ -k. If no such operation exists, return nothing.

The identity is returned if k ≡ -k, i.e., at time-reversal invariant momenta; otherwise, the first such operation of the space group (in the order of spacegroup) is returned. For a spinful (double) little group, g₀ is an operation of the double space group.

Technically, 𝒯g₀, with 𝒯 denoting time-reversal, is a coset representative of the antiunitary coset of the gray little group M(k) = G(k) + 𝒯g₀G(k), with G(k) the (unitary) little group.
